Four reasons you'll never go back.

1

Read it like an editor.

Mark what sings, what drags and what's in the wrong place. Right on the page.

2

Rewrite without fear.

Try a new version right beside the old one. Every version you replace is kept.

3

Kill your darlings. Keep the bodies.

Cut scenes go in the drawer, not the bin. Bring them back whenever you like.

4

Then just write.

One key, and everything disappears except your words.
